**The schema is the source of truth (SDD).** `plugin.go` reads only `kernel` and `rootDrive`, because those are the two things a Firecracker guest boots from, and the schema declares exactly those two. An earlier revision of this work declared `vcpus?` and `memMiB?` as well; **those two fields were removed in this branch, because the launch command is composed with `--kernel` and `--rootfs` only and the CLI has no geometry flags — a declared-but-unhonoured field is a false promise carrying a schema's authority.**
